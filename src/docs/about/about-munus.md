Munus Shih (he/they) is a Taiwanese Minnan-Hakka designer, educator, and researcher based in New York. As an [assistant professor in Graduate Communications Design at Pratt Institute](https://www.pratt.edu/people/munus-shih/), Munus's work explores how designers and communities can collectively own and shape the technologies and systems they use.

Their projects include [BeginContour](https://medium.com/processing-foundation/begincontour-%E5%BE%9E%E7%AD%89%E9%AB%98%E7%B7%9A%E9%96%8B%E5%A7%8B-a-critical-anti-colonial-and-intersectional-pedagogy-to-taiwanese-creative-f44d67fd0ccb) (2022), a teaching practicum, and the open-source publishing tools [p5.genzine](https://parsons.edu/dt-2023/p5-genzine/) (2023) and [p5.book](https://p5-book.vercel.app/) (2026) within the p5.js ecosystem.

They are a co-founder of [CoAssembly](https://coassembly.work/), Taiwan’s first design cooperative, and co-organizer of [Processing Community Day Taiwan](https://pcd.sponact.org/) and [Other Islands Book Fair](https://otherislands.designfuture.space/). They are part of [NEW INC at the New Museum](https://newmuseum.org/new-inc/) and the [Platform Cooperativism Consortium](https://platform.coop/people/munus-shih/), and were previously a [Processing Foundation Fellow](https://processingfoundation.org/fellowships/fellowships-2022) and Designer in Residence.

As a teacher, Munus has taught classes at [Parsons](https://ci2-munus.munusandniktari.com/), [Cooper Union](https://adpd.munusandniktari.com/assets/Makagiansar_Shih_FA-315B-1-Adv.%20Design_%20Product%20Design-2024SP.pdf), and the [School for Poetic Computation](https://sfpc.study/sessions/summer-26/building-benches-for-the-web). As a designer, Munus has worked with AIGA, [Robert Blackburn Printmaking Workshop](https://www.rbpmw-efanyc.org/oralhistory), the [Gallery of Contemporary Art](https://danielirizarry.design/projects/goca), and [The New York Review of Books](https://1883magazine.com/munus-shih-on-capturing-the-new-york-design-aesthetic-a-fusion-of-code-culture-and-creativity/), and has been featured and showcased at [NYU ITP](https://tisch.nyu.edu/itp/events/spring-2023/itp-ima-thursday-night-talk-p5-js-Community-Salon.html), [Harvard GSD](https://www.harvarduxd.com/cast), [National Taiwan University](https://arts.ntu.edu.tw/Event.aspx?ID=1f6ee4b8-49d6-4b56-93ac-43e79391e624&PID=03a80c27-fc26-462f-9355-86cbb89c747e), [Typographics](https://2025.typographics.com/typelab/), the [Open Source Arts Contributor Conference](https://github.com/processing/OSACC-p5.js-Access-Report/blob/main/4-reflection/reflection-by-munus-shih.md), [Taiwan’s Hakka Affairs Council](/guansi/), and [Awwwards](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/r-m).
